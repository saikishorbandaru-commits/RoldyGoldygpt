# RoldyGoldy UI/UX Update

## Updated design direction
- Deep burgundy / crimson luxury base
- Premium gold accents and warm cream typography
- More playful category discovery and micro-interactions
- Product-first e-commerce cards with quick wishlist, bargain and bag actions
- Stronger emphasis on the platform's signature flows: Trial @Home and Exchange & Save
- Improved mobile-first spacing, rounded cards and persistent navigation

## Updated primary experience
1. Location + Trial availability ribbon
2. Premium brand hero with two primary actions
3. Search with Trial-only toggle
4. Visual category discovery
5. Signature service cards
6. Artisan / offer banner area
7. Modern product cards
8. Trust and convenience strip

## Existing functionality retained
- Authentication and OTP demo flow
- Product details
- Wishlist
- Cart and checkout
- Trial @Home flow
- Scrap exchange flow
- Bargain flow
- Order success and reports
- Location selector
- Boutique / studio view
- Account and support flows

The app was visually refactored without removing the existing interaction architecture.
